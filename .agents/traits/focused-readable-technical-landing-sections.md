---
name: focused-readable-technical-landing-sections
subject: Explanatory sections on technical product landing pages.
requirement: Present one clear mechanism or code-to-result story at a time with
  a readable visual hierarchy. Use short, meaningful, syntax-highlighted code
  excerpts and typography and spacing that remain legible at normal desktop and
  mobile sizes. Do not turn an explanatory illustration into a dense miniature
  dashboard with competing panels, tiny labels, excessive controls or nested
  scrolling. Use concrete factual copy; omit decorative eyebrow labels,
  repetitive subtext and generic rhetorical slogans that add no product
  information.
applicability: Applies to technical product landing-page explanations, not
  actual operator dashboards or complete reference documentation. Retain
  secondary text and controls when they materially explain the mechanism,
  necessary limitations or accessibility. Visual inspiration does not authorize
  copying another site's content or assets.
purpose: Make technical capabilities immediately understandable without visual
  clutter, unreadable snippets or formulaic marketing filler.
---
For effect-jobs, use the clarity of the existing Getting Started visuals and the short highlighted-code-plus-focused-animation compositions on alchemy.run as references. Rejected examples include the decorative label DURABLE EXECUTION · NATIVE EFFECT and the generic slogan Your request ends. The work goes on. This does not prescribe one palette, font or layout for every proposal.
