---
title: Durable jobs, explicit ownership
description: Move slow work out of a request without losing its connection to your application transaction.
---

Placing an order should return quickly. Sending its confirmation can take longer,
fail or finish after the requester leaves. effect-jobs keeps that work durable while
your application owns the database, handlers and worker lifetime.

## Build an order workflow

1. [Understand the problem](/docs/start/why-background-jobs) — what durable work solves.
2. [Install the supported stack](/docs/start/installation) and [run your first job](/docs/start/first-job).
3. [Build an order workflow](/docs/start/tutorial), then make its [application write and enqueue atomic](/docs/persistence/transactions).
4. [Run scoped workers](/docs/execution/workers) and distinguish [retries](/docs/execution/retries) from [uncertain outcomes](/docs/execution/recovery).

## Current alpha

This alpha targets Effect 4.0.5, PostgreSQL, and explicitly scoped Node/Bun workers.
Applications own migrations, pools and external-effect idempotency. Durable retries
and fencing do not promise exactly-once external delivery.
See [installation](/docs/start/installation) for exact supported versions and
qualification boundaries. [Explore the read-only demo](/demo) or [return home](/).
